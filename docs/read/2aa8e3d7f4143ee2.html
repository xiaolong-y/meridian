Quantum Computing Inc. Reports Q3 2026 Financial Results: Revenue Reaches $9.5M, Surpassing First-Half Total on Acquired Photonics and Semiconductor Integration

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 11h quantum 814 words 5 min read

Quantum Computing Inc. (Nasdaq: QUBT) has announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Hoboken, New Jersey-based integrated quantum photonics and nanosemiconductor company reported accelerating top-line growth, generating more revenue in Q3 than during the entire first half of 2026 combined. Performance was driven by the commercial integration of its acquisitions—Luminar Semiconductor Inc. (LSI), NuCrypt, and NHanced Semiconductors—while the company maintained a debt-free balance sheet with approximately $1.3 billion in total cash, investments, and accrued interest reserves.
The table below summarizes key GAAP financial metrics for Q3 2026 compared with the prior quarter (Q2 2026) and the year-ago quarter (Q3 2025).

Financial Highlights & Capital Position
QCi generated $9.50 million in revenue for Q3 2026, marking a 71.1% sequential increase over Q2 2026 ($5.55M) and a substantial expansion from $384 thousand in Q3 2025. Third-quarter revenue exceeded the $9.24 million generated across the entire first half of 2026. Top-line expansion was primarily driven by commercial deliveries of photonic components, quantum communications hardware, and advanced semiconductor packaging services to aerospace, government, and industrial customers following the integration of LSI, NuCrypt, and NHanced.
Gross profit totaled $428 thousand, representing a gross margin of 4.5%, as the company continues to scale foundry operations and optimize capacity utilization across its fabrication facilities.
Operating expenses for the quarter rose to $29.01 million, compared to $21.85 million in Q2 2026 and $10.53 million in Q3 2025. The year-over-year increase was driven by expanded research and development personnel, scaling commercial go-to-market teams, and approximately $1.0 million in acquisition-related transaction and integration expenses. Operating loss widened to ($28.59) million.
GAAP Net Loss for Q3 2026 was ($15.15) million, or ($0.07) per basic and diluted share, compared to net income of $2.38 million in Q3 2025 (which had been elevated by a $9.25M warrant derivative fair-value gain). The operating loss was partially offset below the line by $13.05 million in interest and other income earned on the company’s $1.31 billion liquid asset base and a $458 thousand derivative liability fair-value gain.
Contract backlog stood at $39.1 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $42.5 million at the end of Q2 2026, reflecting contract execution and revenue conversion during the period.
Commercial Milestones & Academic Collaborations
- First Pharmaceutical Commercial Contract: Entered into a commercial agreement with INmune Bio to apply QCi’s Dirac quantum optimization platform, combined with AI and conventional data analytics, to clinical and biomedical datasets for investigational Alzheimer’s disease therapeutics. This marks QCi’s initial commercial deployment within the biopharmaceutical sector.
- International Academic Partnership: Signed a framework agreement with Hamad Bin Khalifa University in Qatar to advance quantum computing research, education, and workforce training. The collaboration provides cloud-based access to QCi’s Dirac-3 entropy quantum optimization platform to investigate optimization, sensing, and quantum-secured communication use cases.
- Commercial Integration of Foundry and Packaging Assets: Advanced the integration of Fab 1 (thin-film lithium niobate / TFLN chip prototyping in Tempe, Arizona) and Fab 2 (advanced packaging and 3D heterogeneous integration in North Carolina and Indiana), expanding customer engagements across commercial defense and aerospace supply chains.
Technology Roadmap & Manufacturing Progress
- Dirac Platform Benchmarking: Progressed toward publishing head-to-head benchmarking results for the 10,000-variable Dirac-3 system against classical mathematical solvers, validating the capabilities of room-temperature entropy quantum optimization for complex logistics and financial portfolio modeling.
- Photonic Edge AI (NeuraWave): Continued commercialization of its NeuraWave photonic reservoir computing platform following its deployment-readiness milestone and framework agreement with Planck Dynamics for high-efficiency AI inference.
- TFLN Process Development: Advanced wafer-scale process development for proprietary thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) optical chips, targeting complete on-chip detector integration and hybrid 3D integration with silicon photonics to support telecommunications and quantum-secure communications (QKD) hardware.

QuSecure’s QuProtect R3 PQC Platform Added to Carahsoft’s GSA Schedule Contract

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 8h quantum 354 words 2 min read

Post-quantum cryptography (PQC) and crypto-agility software provider QuSecure, Inc. and government IT aggregator Carahsoft Technology Corp. have announced the addition of QuSecure’s QuProtect R3 encryption modernization platform to Carahsoft’s GSA Schedule contract (Contract No. 47QSWA18D008F). The contract addition simplifies procurement for U.S. federal defense, intelligence, and civilian agencies seeking to meet federal post-quantum migration mandates.
The addition addresses federal timelines set by executive orders and National Security Memorandum 10 (NSM-10), which mandate that high-value federal information systems upgrade key establishment protocols by December 31, 2030, and digital signature algorithms by December 31, 2031. Operating as an external cryptographic control plane, QuProtect R3 enables security administrators to swap encryption algorithms and enforce policy-based crypto-agility across legacy, cloud, and air-gapped environments without altering underlying application source code or triggering infrastructure “rip-and-replace” cycles.

QuProtect R3 unifies three core modules into a single control layer:
- Reconnaissance (Discovery): Conducts live packet inspection and continuous discovery to maintain an active inventory of cryptographic assets across network backplanes. Quantum Computing Report
- Resilience (Remediation): Enables dynamic algorithm swapping and crypto-agile policy updates without system downtime or code refactoring. Quantum Computing Report
- Reporting (Governance): Automatically compiles audit-ready telemetry and generates single-click Cryptographic Bill of Materials (CBOM) outputs aligned with NSA CNSA 2.0, CNSSP 15, FIPS, and GDPR standards. Quantum Computing Report
In addition to the GSA Schedule, QuSecure’s solutions are distributed through Carahsoft across multiple public sector contract vehicles, including NASA SEWP V (Contracts NNG15SC03B and NNG15SC27B), U.S. Army ITES-SW2 (Contract W52P1J-20-D-0042), NASPO ValuePoint (Master Agreement #AR2472), and OMNIA Partners (Contract #R240303).

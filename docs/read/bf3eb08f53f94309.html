Black Kite Introduces Quantum Readiness Controls to Assess Third-Party Cryptographic Risk

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 1d quantum 358 words 2 min read

Third-party cyber risk management platform provider Black Kite has announced the launch of Quantum Readiness, a new control set integrated into the SSL/TLS Strength category of its Black Kite Cyber Rating engine to evaluate post-quantum cryptographic (PQC) agility across third-party vendor supply chains.
The feature provides organizations with external visibility into vendor cryptographic postures, identifying which third-party software, cloud, and IT providers have implemented quantum-resistant encryption methods—such as hybrid post-quantum key exchange and PQC certificate signature readiness—or remain reliant on legacy algorithms facing regulatory phase-outs. The rollout addresses mounting regulatory compliance deadlines, including U.S. Office of Management and Budget (OMB) M-26-15 guidelines mandating post-quantum key establishment by 2030 and digital signatures by 2031, Executive Order 14412 contractor migration targets, and European Union NIS2 supply chain cryptographic mandates.

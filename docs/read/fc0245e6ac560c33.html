Quantinuum and The University of Western Australia Partner to Advance Australia’s Hybrid Quantum-AI-HPC Ecosystem

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 1d quantum 390 words 2 min read

Trapped-ion quantum computing developer Quantinuum (NASDAQ: QNT) has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with The University of Western Australia (UWA) to expand quantum research, workforce training, and industrial application development across Australia. The partnership provides UWA-affiliated researchers, students, and startups with cloud-based access to Quantinuum’s full-stack quantum ecosystem, including its flagship 98-qubit Helios trapped-ion system built on the Quantum Charge-Coupled Device (QCCD) architecture.
The collaboration focuses on co-designing hybrid workflows that integrate quantum computing, artificial intelligence, and high-performance computing (HPC). Joint teams will evaluate technical benchmarks and algorithm performance across key Australian economic sectors, including critical minerals, energy, agriculture, and healthcare. The agreement also includes plans to explore establishing a university-wide quantum computing applications centre led by UWA’s Research Centre for Quantum Information, Simulation and Algorithm (QUISA).

Through Quantinuum’s global Q-Net network and Startup Partner Program, Western Australian researchers and entrepreneurs will receive hands-on training and access to software stacks such as InQuanto and TKET. The agreement aligns with broader national quantum initiatives supported by Quantum Australia, facilitating international collaboration and technical integration across global supply chains.

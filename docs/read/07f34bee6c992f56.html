Wultra Secures €6.8 Million ($7.7 Million USD) Series A to Expand Post-Quantum Digital Identity Infrastructure Globally

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 15h quantum 486 words 3 min read

Prague-based cybersecurity provider Wultra has closed a €6.8 million ($7.7 million USD) Series A funding round to scale its digital identity platform and fast-track the international expansion of its post-quantum authentication stack. The investment round was led by French venture capital firm Seventure Partners, with additional capital injections from ARIADNEXT co-founders Marc Norlain and Guillaume Despagne, alongside returning institutional backers J&T Ventures and Elevator Ventures (the venture arm of Raiffeisen Bank International). The fresh capital reserves will be used to grow headcount, secure large strategic financial customers, and extend the firm’s sovereign infrastructure footprint from its active regional hub in Singapore directly into the United States and Middle East fintech ecosystems.
The capital expansion occurs as global banking networks, public institutions, and fintech enterprises accelerate their transitions to long-term cryptographic resilience. Financial entities face immediate vulnerabilities from highly sophisticated, AI-enabled identity fraud—such as deepfakes and synthetic media—while concurrently rushing to replace legacy public key infrastructures before quantum decryption capabilities mature. Driven by the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) publishing official post-quantum cryptography standards and rigid enterprise migration mandates set by global regulators, Wultra’s software platform secures the entire digital user journey, embedding quantum-resistant cryptography directly across customer onboarding, identity proofing, multi-factor login authorization, and qualified electronic signatures.

A core technical priority for the Series A proceeds is the engineering acceleration of Wultra’s proprietary digital identity wallet capabilities. This development framework is intentionally optimized to support the large-scale rollout of the upcoming European Digital Identity Wallet network, ensuring native compliance with stringent regional directives such as eIDAS 2.0, PSD3, and PSR1. By replacing vulnerable authentication protocols with phishing-resistant, passwordless passkeys based on the FIDO2 standard, the deep-tech firm’s platform isolates banking infrastructure from credential theft and authorized push payment fraud while providing financial-grade transaction signing.
The software vendor’s market traction is highlighted by a multi-national footprint supporting more than 70 clients across 25 countries. The startup achieved early validation after being recognized as a Sample Vendor for Post-Quantum Authentication within the Gartner Hype Cycle for Digital Identity. Having expanded its baseline engineering team by nearly 50% over the last twelve months, the Prague-based firm intends to position its platform as a mandatory, plug-and-play compliance layer for international corporate clients looking to transition traditional web browsers, cloud edge terminals, and mobile applications onto verified quantum-safe communication channels.

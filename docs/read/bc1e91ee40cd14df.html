IQM Quantum Computers Sells 54-Qubit Radiance R3 System to South Korean Quantum-AI Infrastructure Firm QAI

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 13h quantum 299 words 2 min read

IQM Quantum Computers (Nasdaq: IQMX) has received an order valued between EUR 8 million and EUR 10 million from South Korean data center and cloud infrastructure company QAI Co., Ltd. for an on-premises 54-qubit IQM Radiance R3 superconducting quantum computer. Scheduled for delivery and installation at QAI’s private AI data center in Seoul by the end of 2027, the agreement marks IQM’s first deployment into a private commercial data center in Asia and its second system sale in South Korea following an earlier IQM Spark installation at Chungbuk National University.
Designed for low-latency integration alongside classical high-performance computing clusters, the 54-qubit Radiance R3 features tunable couplers arranged in a square-lattice topology that natively supports surface-code error correction, pulse-level access, and modular component upgrades. QAI plans to couple the processor with its GPU-based AI infrastructure and offer domestic access to enterprises, universities, and public organizations through its Quantum-as-a-Service (QaaS) cloud platform across drug discovery, battery materials, financial optimization, and logistics scheduling.
The deployment aligns with South Korea’s national quantum strategy under the Ministry of Science and ICT, which targets broad domestic quantum computing utilization by 2035 alongside expanded industry and workforce development.

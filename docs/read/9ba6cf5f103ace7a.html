Quantum eMotion Submits Quantum Entropy Source for NIST SP 800-90B Validation

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 4h quantum 355 words 2 min read

Quantum cybersecurity developer Quantum eMotion Corp. (NYSE American: QNC; TSXV: QNC; FSE: 34Q0) has submitted its eCore-Q PCIe Quantum Entropy Module for official validation under the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cryptographic Module Validation Program (CMVP). The validation testing package was independently assessed and submitted by cybersecurity laboratory Lightship Security Inc. through the production Entropy Source Validation Test System (ESVTS).

NIST SP 800-90B Standard & FIPS 140-3 Integration
NIST SP 800-90B establishes the rigorous mathematical and statistical requirements for validating non-deterministic random number generators (NRNGs) used across federal and high-security cryptographic infrastructure.
- Independent Verification: Lightship Security characterized, evaluated, and documented the physical unpredictability of QeM’s electron-tunneling Quantum Random Number Generator (QRNG) hardware to confirm compliance with NIST standard test suites.
- Prerequisite for FIPS 140-3: A formal Entropy Validation Certificate issued via the ESVTS is a mandatory building block for downstream FIPS 140-3 cryptographic module certifications. Once validated, system integrators can incorporate the eCore-Q module into regulated hardware products for government, healthcare, and enterprise finance deployments.
- Commercial Portfolio Alignment: The submission directly strengthens Quantum eMotion’s integrated security stack—combining its eFlux-Q entropy stream with its runtime memory protection platform (eShield-Q) and the recently acquired SecureKey™ zero-exposure credential architecture.
Led by CEO Francis Bellido, the Montreal-based company expects the CMVP review process to advance its market access across North American defense, cloud IT, and critical infrastructure sectors.

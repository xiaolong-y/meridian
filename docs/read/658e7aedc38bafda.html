Quantum Computing Inc. Signs Three-Year Framework Agreement with Hamad Bin Khalifa University for Middle East QPU Deployment

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 1d quantum 367 words 2 min read

Integrated photonics and quantum hardware manufacturer Quantum Computing Inc. (NASDAQ: QUBT) has executed a three-year commercial and academic Framework Agreement with Hamad Bin Khalifa University (HBKU) in Qatar. Operating through HBKU’s Qatar Center for Quantum Computing (QC2), the strategic initiative covers joint research, workforce training, and regional technology deployment across quantum computing, quantum sensing, and quantum communications.
Under the terms of the agreement, QCi will provide remote cloud access to its Dirac-3 Entropy Quantum Computer alongside an on-premises hardware installation in Qatar. The room-temperature, photonics-based Dirac-3 platform utilizes non-equilibrium open-system dynamics to solve dense discrete optimization problems, bypassing the cryogenic overhead associated with superconducting or trapped-ion architectures. HBKU will integrate the platform into its existing quantum infrastructure—which includes Qatar’s first national quantum-safe QKD communication link—to benchmark optimization algorithms for energy, logistics, and government applications across the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) region.

The collaboration supports QCi’s commercial footprint in the Middle East while leveraging HBKU’s position under the Qatar Foundation to accelerate regional technology adoption. The contract expands QCi’s external hardware deployments following commercial engagements for its Dirac-3 platform across biomedical, defense, and industrial logistics domains.

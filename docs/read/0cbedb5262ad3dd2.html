Who’s News: Strategic Leadership Appointments at PsiQuantum, Q-CAB, Q.ANT, enQase, IQMP, and IonQ

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 2d quantum 594 words 3 min read

PsiQuantum has announced the appointment of Lip-Bu Tan to its Board of Directors. Tan, a titan of the semiconductor industry and current CEO of Intel Corporation, brings decades of experience in building and scaling foundational computing technologies. Formerly the CEO of Cadence Design Systems, Tan’s expertise in electronic design automation and global semiconductor ecosystems will support PsiQuantum as it scales its silicon photonics platform and deploys utility-scale quantum facilities in Chicago and Brisbane. His appointment follows the recent arrival of Victor Peng (former President of AMD) as Interim CEO. The full announcement is available here.
The European Commission has launched the Quantum Computing Advisory Board (Q-CAB) to provide independent, platform-agnostic expertise on the state of quantum computing in Europe. Chaired by Professor Peter Zoller and coordinated alongside Tommaso Calarco, the board features a “who’s who” of European quantum science, including:
- Prof. David DiVincenzo (Forschungszentrum Jülich)
- Dr. Michael Bolle (Carl Zeiss Stiftung)
- Prof. Sabrina Maniscalco (University of Helsinki)
- Prof. Nikolay V. Vitanov (Sofia University)
- Prof. Ignacio Cirac (Max Planck Institute)
- Mr. Olivier Ezratty (Quantum Energy Initiative) Q-CAB will advise on the industrialization of the quantum value chain and help define investment and procurement roadmaps as the field moves toward fault-tolerant systems. The full announcement is available here.
Q.ANT has announced its expansion into the United States with the opening of a new headquarters in Austin, Texas, and the appointment of semiconductor veteran Bruno Spruth as Chief Technology Officer. Spruth joins the photonic computing pioneer from IBM, where he most recently served as Vice President of Power Processor Development, leading global strategy for one of the world’s most advanced high-performance computing architectures. In his new role, Spruth will oversee Q.ANT’s technological strategy and product development in North America as the company seeks to scale its energy-efficient photonic processors within the U.S. hyperscale and AI markets. The full announcement is available here.
enQase has welcomed Mahi Dontamsetti to its Advisory Board. Dontamsetti is a recognized authority in global financial risk, having previously served as the Chief Technology Risk Officer (CTO) at State Street, where he managed risk for over $46 trillion in assets. In coordination with his appointment, enQase is launching “The CTRO Viewpoint,” a new content series beginning with Dontamsetti’s feature article, “Beyond the Silos: Lessons from the Frontlines of a Chief Technology Risk Officer.” The series addresses the urgency of quantum-safe migration, cryptographic agility, and the integration of technical risk into boardroom strategy. The full announcement is available here, and the inaugural feature can be read here.
Illinois Quantum and Microelectronics Park (IQMP) has announced the appointment of Oscar Bedolla as its new Chief Strategy Officer. Bedolla joins the 128-acre South Chicago development from Amazon International Corporate Finance, where he served as Head of Insights, Performance, Analytics, and Technology. With over 20 years of experience in economic development and strategic planning—including previous leadership roles at KPMG and the City of Cincinnati—Bedolla will oversee the alignment of public and private stakeholders to drive the industrial-scale growth of the Park. The full announcement is available here.
IonQ (NYSE: IONQ) continues its aggressive talent acquisition with the appointment of Jeff Henshaw as SVP of Quantum Computing Products. Henshaw, a seasoned technology executive, joins the team after years of advising various startups and established firms across the quantum ecosystem. In this new role, he will focus on accelerating the delivery of IonQ’s product roadmap and scaling system value for commercial customers. The welcome announcement can be found here.
We wish all these organizations and individuals success in their new endeavors.

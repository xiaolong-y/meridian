Patero and Horizen Labs Partner on Enterprise Post-Quantum Migration and Network Protection

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 19h quantum 358 words 2 min read

Post-quantum security provider Patero and cryptography advisory firm Horizen Labs have announced a commercial collaboration to assist enterprises in transitioning digital communications to quantum-resistant encryption. The partnership combines Horizen Labs’ quantum risk advisory services with Patero’s automated discovery and hybrid encryption platforms to help organizations sequence migrations and validate quantum-resilient controls on live infrastructure.

Transitioning enterprise IT architectures to quantum-safe standards presents significant engineering friction, as legacy public-key algorithms are deeply embedded across distributed services, operational pipelines, and third-party software. Without end-to-end estate mapping, organizations struggle to determine which cryptographic assets are exposed to intercept-now, decrypt-later risks or identify where protocol changes could trigger system outages.
The joint engagement model structures client readiness into defined phases. Horizen Labs establishes the risk governance framework, determining which business operations require immediate cryptographic agility and defining performance criteria for live-traffic trials. Technical teams then conduct pilot runs on production routes, validating interoperability and ensuring verifiable rollback paths before committing capital to enterprise-wide infrastructure overhauls.
Patero is based within the Quantum Startup Foundry at the University of Maryland, College Park. Details on the partnership are available via the joint press release here.

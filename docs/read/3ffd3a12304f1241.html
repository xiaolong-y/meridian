Keyfactor Surpasses $200 Million ARR Amid Enterprise Post-Quantum Migration Surge

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 23h quantum 371 words 2 min read

Machine identity security and cryptography management platform Keyfactor has announced that its annual recurring revenue (ARR) surpassed $200 million at the beginning of Q3 2026. Doubling its revenue scale in under two years after crossing $100 million ARR, the milestone highlights accelerating commercial demand for enterprise trust infrastructure as public sector agencies and multinational corporations prepare for the transition to post-quantum cryptography (PQC).
The company’s sustained 35% year-over-year revenue expansion is bolstered by major institutional transactions. In July 2026, Keyfactor secured a $1 billion-plus strategic growth investment led by Summit Partners to expand its global footprint and accelerate acquisitions. Additionally, the firm announced its intent to acquire UK-based Cofide to integrate SPIFFE/SPIRE-based workload identity verification for cloud native environments and AI agents directly into its Trust Control Plane.

Demand for Keyfactor’s platform is driven by tightening regulatory mandates, including the White House’s June 2026 executive orders requiring federal agencies to expedite PQC migration ahead of the 2030 deadline. Operating with FedRAMP certification status, Keyfactor equips government agencies, defense units across NATO member states, and commercial institutions with automated certificate discovery, crypto-agility frameworks, and cloud-native Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) required to replace vulnerable classical algorithms.
Through its expanded global partner ecosystem, Keyfactor enables systems integrators and security providers to deploy PQC Centers of Excellence, helping enterprises audit cryptographic assets across hybrid environments and establish post-quantum trust architectures.

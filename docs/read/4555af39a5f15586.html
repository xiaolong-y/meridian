D-Wave Reports Q3 2026 Results: Revenue Up 21% YoY, RPO Surges 1,221% to $38.3M, and Finalizes $100M CHIPS Act Agreement

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 6h quantum 991 words 5 min read

D-Wave Quantum Inc. (NYSE: QBTS) has announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Palo Alto, California-based developer highlighted growing commercial adoption of its dual-platform approach—spanning production annealing quantum systems and dual-rail superconducting gate-model quantum computers—underpinned by a surge in multiquarter bookings, production-grade enterprise deployments, and the execution of a definitive $100 million CHIPS Act funding agreement.

Financial Performance & Commercial Backlog
D-Wave generated $4.51 million in revenue for Q3 2026, up 20.7% year-over-year compared to $3.74 million in Q3 2025. Top-line growth was driven by an expansion in professional services revenue (up $1.5 million to $2.0 million) and Quantum Computing as a Service (QCaaS) revenue (up $0.8 million to $2.2 million), which offset a $1.5 million year-over-year reduction in upfront system sales revenue.
GAAP gross profit was $2.39 million (52.9% gross margin), compared to $2.67 million (71.4% margin) in Q3 2025, with the margin compression reflecting the higher proportion of professional services alongside an increase in non-cash stock compensation. On a non-GAAP basis, Non-GAAP Gross Profit was $2.87 million (63.6% margin).
Operating expenses totaled $55.87 million in Q3 2026, up 83.7% year-over-year from $30.41 million, driven by engineering and headcount additions following the Quantum Circuits acquisition, $10.22 million in operating stock-based compensation, and $4.91 million in depreciation and amortization. Operating loss widened to ($53.48) million.
GAAP Net Loss was ($48.20) million, or ($0.13) per share, narrowing substantially from a net loss of ($139.99) million, or ($0.41) per share, in Q3 2025. This bottom-line improvement was driven by the elimination of non-cash warrant liability remeasurement charges, which had added $121.86 million to net loss in Q3 2025 prior to the full redemption of all publicly traded warrants in November 2025. Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA Loss stood at ($37.66) million.
Multi-Year Booking Momentum
- Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO): Surged 1,221% year-over-year to $38.3 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $2.9 million at the end of Q3 2025. Approximately 59% of the balance is scheduled for revenue conversion over the next 12 months, and 71% over the next 24 months.
- Year-to-Date Bookings: Bookings for the first nine months of 2026 reached $37.4 million, up 611% year-over-year from $5.3 million in 9M 2025.
- Customer Base: D-Wave recognized revenue from more than 100 customers in Q3 (and over 125 customers across 9M 2026), with 48% representing commercial enterprise accounts.
Definitive $100M CHIPS and Science Act Agreement
D-Wave finalized a definitive agreement with the U.S. Department of Commerce providing access to up to $100 million in milestone-based funding under the U.S. CHIPS and Science Act. Under the terms of the agreement, the Commerce Department received a minority, non-controlling equity stake in D-Wave.
The capital will fund research, fabrication, and packaging across both arms of D-Wave’s hardware roadmap:
- 100,000-Qubit Annealing System: Expanding beyond the current 4,400+ qubit Advantage2 platform toward 20,000-qubit (Advantage3) and 100,000-qubit processors utilizing multi-chip packaging and advanced superconducting interconnect fabrics.
- 10,000-Qubit Gate-Model System: Advancing D-Wave’s dual-rail superconducting architecture toward a 10,000-qubit machine designed to yield 100 fault-tolerant logical qubits capable of executing over 1 million quantum logic gates.
Commercial & Operational Milestones
- NTT DOCOMO Production Deployment: Japan’s largest mobile network operator deployed its second production application powered by D-Wave hybrid quantum solvers. The production application optimized tracking area lists across 333 base stations in five minutes, reducing daily peak location registration signals by 65.3% and paging signals by 7.0%.
- System Delivery in Italy: Shipped an Advantage2 annealing quantum computer (4,400+ qubits) to Villa Saporiti in Como, Italy, funded by Swiss Quantum Technology SA to anchor the Q-Alliance regional quantum ecosystem.
- Gate-Model Simulator Beta Launch: Rolled out the beta program for its dual-rail superconducting gate-model simulator, enabling enterprise partners—including BBVA, FirstQFM, Florida Atlantic University, and the Jülich Supercomputing Centre—to prototype error-aware quantum algorithms.
- SkyWater Manufacturing Extension & Fab Optimization: Executed a multi-year extension with SkyWater Technology to fabricate custom superconducting quantum processors. Concurrently, D-Wave completed a proof-of-technology project deploying quantum annealing to optimize maintenance scheduling inside SkyWater’s semiconductor fab, reducing wafer lot delays by up to 30%.
- Enterprise Channel Partnerships: Formed a global systems integration alliance with CGI to bring hybrid quantum solvers to logistics and retail enterprise clients, and signed an MOU with South Korea’s LG CNS for mathematical optimization services.
- Board Appointments: Appointed former BNP Paribas Group CIO Bernard Gavgani to the Cybersecurity Committee and veteran finance executive Kevan Krysler to the Audit Committee.

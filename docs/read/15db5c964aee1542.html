Neutral-Atom Developer Oratomic Secures $775 Million in Cumulative Funding for Fault-Tolerant System R&D

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 12h quantum 261 words 2 min read

Pasadena-based quantum hardware developer Oratomic has closed $775 million in cumulative funding since emerging in March 2026. Led by ARCH Venture Partners, Spark Capital, Khosla Ventures, Index Ventures, General Catalyst, and Bezos Expeditions, the capital will be directed toward expanding the company’s scientific and engineering workforce, cleanroom footprint, and laboratory testing infrastructure in California. Bypassing intermediate Noisy Intermediate-Scale Quantum (NISQ) commercialization, Oratomic operates exclusively on a fault-tolerant technical roadmap utilizing reconfigurable neutral-atom arrays held in optical tweezers paired with quantum error correction (QEC) protocols.

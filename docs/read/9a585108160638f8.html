Rigetti Computing Integrates Quantum Elements Constellation Platform into Error Correction Pipeline

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 19h quantum 340 words 2 min read

Superconducting quantum computer developer Rigetti Computing (NASDAQ: RGTI) has integrated Quantum Elements’ Constellation simulation environment into its quantum error correction (QEC) workflow to model processor behavior and project system performance at scale. Following a three-year technical collaboration, Rigetti validated the platform’s ability to predict single- and two-qubit gate fidelity across its 36-qubit and 108-qubit quantum processing units (QPUs).

Scaling superconducting quantum processors toward fault tolerance requires detailed characterization of device physics before committing expensive fabrication or cryogenic runtime cycles. By running physically accurate digital twins on classical hardware, Constellation allows engineering teams to evaluate pulse schedules and system dynamics without consuming physical QPU time.
According to Rigetti Chief Executive Officer Subodh Kulkarni, the joint effort expanded the company’s modeling reach across multi-chip architectures, giving hardware and error-correction teams a unified benchmark for diagnosing performance-limiting mechanisms as qubit counts grow.

Infleqtion Reports Q2 2026 Results: Record Revenue Up 116% YoY, Raised Guidance to $43M, and $100M CHIPS Act LOI

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 18h quantum 653 words 4 min read

Infleqtion, Inc. (NYSE: INFQ) has announced its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026. The Louisville-based neutral-atom quantum computing and quantum sensing leader delivered record quarterly performance driven by 100% organic growth across its dual computing and sensing verticals.
The table below summarizes key GAAP financial metrics for Q2 2026 compared with the prior quarter (Q1 2026) and the year-ago quarter (Q2 2025).

| Revenue | $12.63 | $9.46 | $5.84 | +33.5% | +116.3% |
| Operating Expenses | $32.49 | $31.72 | $11.56 | +2.4% | +181.1% |

Financial Highlights & Raised FY 2026 Guidance
Infleqtion generated record quarterly revenue of $12.63 million, representing a 116.3% increase year-over-year ($5.84M in Q2 2025) and a 33.5% sequential gain over Q1 2026 ($9.46M). Top-line expansion was entirely organic and derived 100% from quantum hardware, software, and sensing deployments.
Operating expenses for the quarter totaled $32.49 million ($12.68M R&D, $19.82M SG&A, offset by $0.47M grant income). GAAP Net Loss was ($25.47) million, or ($0.12) per share, compared to ($8.85) million in Q2 2025. On a non-GAAP basis, Non-GAAP Loss from Operations stood at ($16.98) million, excluding $12.07M in stock-based compensation and transaction-related expenses.
Driven by strong customer demand, Infleqtion raised its full-year 2026 revenue outlook to approximately $43 million.
Strategic U.S. Government LOI ($100M CHIPS Act Proposed Funding)
The U.S. Department of Commerce selected Infleqtion for a Letter of Intent (LOI) providing up to $100 million in proposed funding under CHIPS Act programs to accelerate neutral-atom commercialization following a technical review of the company’s hardware roadmap. The proposed funding contemplates the Department of Commerce receiving common stock in Infleqtion.
Computing Roadmap & Commercial Deployments
- Logical Qubit Target: On track to demonstrate 30 logical qubits in 2026, with defined circuits for enterprise customer workloads across energy, finance, and precision medicine.
- Illinois Quantum Computer Contract: Under contract to deploy a neutral-atom quantum computer at the Illinois Quantum & Microelectronics Park in 2027. The system features a new modular architecture designed to scale past 50 logical qubits toward a long-term goal of 100 logical qubits.
- Energy Sector Commercialization: Enterprise client Eaton is utilizing private-cloud access to Infleqtion’s Sqale system for complex energy grid applications.
- DOE Genesis Mission Wins: Selected for three U.S. Department of Energy Genesis Mission projects spanning AI, nuclear applications, and fusion research.
- Executive Appointment: Appointed Dr. Joseph Buck (former Lockheed Martin Senior Fellow) as Senior Vice President of Quantum Computing Systems to lead Sqale development.
Quantum Sensing & Defense Infrastructure
- NASA Quantum Gravity Gradiometer: Execution on NASA’s gravity gradiometry program served as a primary revenue driver in Q2.
- Tiqker Atomic Clocks: Commercializing its third-generation Tiqker optical atomic clock systems supported by a global co-selling partnership with Safran.
- Congressional Testimony: CEO Matt Kinsella testified before the U.S. House Committee on Natural Resources regarding the Quantum-Enhanced Critical Minerals Mapping Act of 2026.

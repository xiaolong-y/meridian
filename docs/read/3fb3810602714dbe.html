EMode Photonix Raises $1M Pre-Seed Round Led by Ground State Ventures for Photonic Circuit Simulation Stack

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 1d quantum 428 words 3 min read

Boulder, Colorado-based quantum integrated photonics software startup EMode Photonix has announced the closure of a $1 million pre-seed funding round led by Ground State Ventures, with participation from Harlow Capital Management.
Co-founded in 2021 by CEO Eric Stanton (former NIST postdoc and UC Santa Barbara researcher) and Maiya A. S., EMode Photonix has developed a specialized multi-physics simulation platform tailored for non-linear integrated photonic circuits (PICs). Built on proprietary computational modeling techniques, newly derived frequency conversion equations, and a novel 3D method for interfacial scattering loss, the company’s simulation stack achieves up to a 1,000x speedup over traditional non-linear photonics modeling workflows while incorporating coupled thermal, radio-frequency (RF), and non-linear optical dynamics.

The pre-seed investment marks the first institutional capital taken on by the startup following years of organic growth supported by commercial software revenue and competitive federal Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) awards from NASA, NSF, and NIST. EMode’s platform enables photonics engineers to model complex classical and quantum light transformations—such as non-linear frequency conversion, parametric and dissipative effects, and squeezed-light generation—within photonic chips using fabrication-realistic geometries and materials.
The capital will be used to expand the company’s engineering team in Boulder and accelerate the rollout of its upcoming enterprise software offering, which aims to automate functional photonic integrated circuit design within a single day for quantum computing, optical sensing, and telecommunications markets.

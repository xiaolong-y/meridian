Xanadu Reports Q2 2026 Financial Results: $312.8M Cash Position, $67.2M Raised via ATM Facility, and Albany Expansion

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 22h quantum 708 words 4 min read

Xanadu Quantum Technologies Ltd. (NASDAQ/TSX: XNDU) has announced its financial results for the second quarter ended June 30, 2026. The Toronto-based photonic quantum leader highlighted significant hardware and algorithmic progress, expansion of its U.S. operations, and strategic use of its synthetic at-the-market (ATM) equity facility to fortify its balance sheet.
Because of the reverse recapitalization resulting from its Q1 2026 SPAC merger, historical share counts and per-share metrics have been retroactively recast. The table below summarizes key GAAP financial metrics for Q2 2026 compared with the prior quarter (Q1 2026) and the year-ago quarter (Q2 2025).

Financial Results and ATM Equity Facility Utilization
Xanadu ended the second quarter in a strong liquidity position with $312.8 million in cash and cash equivalents, up from $272.5 million at the end of Q1 2026. This increase was primarily driven by the disciplined utilization of its $300 million Standby Equity Purchase Agreement (synthetic ATM facility) established with Yorkville Advisors in May 2026. During Q2, the company raised $67.2 million in net proceeds by issuing 5.5 million Class B subordinate voting shares at an average net price of $12.28 per share.
Second-quarter revenue was $1.51 million, representing a 42.5% increase year-over-year compared to $1.06 million in Q2 2025 (primarily driven by DARPA Stage B contract revenue), though down sequentially from $2.83 million in Q1 2026.
GAAP Net Loss for Q2 2026 expanded to ($42.05) million, or ($0.14) per basic and diluted share, compared to ($20.60) million in Q1 2026 and ($15.14) million in Q2 2025. The widening loss was driven by increased R&D engineering hires ($19.72M vs $17.35M in Q1), higher G&A public company costs ($11.05M), and a $10.60 million non-cash loss on the fair value remeasurement of earn-out share liabilities. On a non-GAAP basis, Adjusted EBITDA loss was ($21.33) million.
Capital expenditures for the quarter rose sharply to $6.4 million (up from $0.3M in Q1 2026), reflecting increased investments in foundry fabrication runs and laboratory infrastructure.
Hardware and Fabrication Breakthroughs
- Edge-Coupling Efficiency: Achieved a record low average edge-coupling loss of 0.085 dB per facet, enabled by internal packaging breakthroughs and manufacturing collaborations with Corning and DISCO.
- Expanded Foundry Runs: Scaled up fabrication runs across its two primary material platforms—increasing thin-film lithium niobate (TFLN) runs by ~75% and silicon nitride (SiN) runs by ~50%.
- U.S. Operational Scaling: Announced a major expansion of U.S. operations centered in Albany, New York. Xanadu’s U.S. headcount has grown more than five-fold since 2023, with continued engineering recruitment planned through the end of 2026.
Software, Algorithms, and Ecosystem Momentum
- QROM Algorithmic Cut: Published a breakthrough in Quantum Read-Only Memory (QROM) that cuts required Toffoli gate operations by roughly 50%. The capability has been patent-filed and integrated directly into PennyLane.
- Ribosomal RNA Model: Successfully trained a Fourier-based quantum machine learning model featuring over one million parameters, demonstrated learning the complex distribution of ribosomal RNA.
- PennyLane & Catalyst Updates: Released PennyLane 0.45 and Catalyst 0.15, while the foundational PennyLane research paper crossed 2,000 academic citations.
- High-Performance & Enterprise Partnerships:
- Oak Ridge National Laboratory: Partnered to deploy PennyLane onto the Frontier exascale supercomputer.
- Rolls-Royce: Re-signed a multi-year partnership focused on quantum computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and aerodynamics.
- Lockheed Martin: Launched a joint quantum machine learning and workforce training initiative under Lockheed’s Quantum Talent Pipeline.
- Financial Sector & Research: Advanced engagements with the Fidelity Center for Applied Technology (FCAT), major banking institutions, Los Alamos National Laboratory, and joined the Unitary Foundation.

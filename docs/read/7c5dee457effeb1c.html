India’s MeitY Sanctions ₹99.94 Crore ($10.3 Million USD) for IIT Madras and izmo Microsystems Silicon Photonics Program

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 10h quantum 224 words 2 min read

Phase II targets the development of a silicon photonics Process Design Kit (PDK) incorporating over 100 IP blocks for 200 mm wafer platforms. While the program addresses high-bandwidth data center interconnects (1.6 Tbps transceivers operating at 1550 nm) and co-packaged optics (CPO) for AI compute clusters, it establishes a dedicated workstream for quantum communications. Technical objectives include chip-scale Quantum Random Number Generators (QRNG) and integrated Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) photonic circuits.
To address commercial deployment challenges in silicon photonics, izmo Microsystems will lead precision assembly, fiber alignment and attachment, electronic-photonic IC (PIC-EIC) co-integration, and thermal packaging. The collaboration aims to build a certified co-packaged optics design rule and packaging service capability, establishing an end-to-end pathway from foundry chip fabrication to qualified system integration across data center, telecommunications, defense, and quantum technology sectors.

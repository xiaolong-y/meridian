Arqit Invests in Sirius to Scale Tactical Edge Quantum-Safe Networking Following NATO Contract Selection

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 2h quantum 517 words 3 min read

Quantum-safe encryption provider Arqit Quantum Inc. (Nasdaq: ARQQ) has announced a strategic investment in tactical communications developer Sirius Computer, Inc., deepening an operational partnership designed to deliver post-quantum security to defense, intelligence, and edge-computing environments. The transaction follows Arqit’s recent selection as part of a winning bidding consortium to supply secure networking services to NATO.
The joint architecture addresses a fundamental vulnerability in military communications: protecting decentralized, autonomous, and mobile assets operating over congested or untrusted radio links. By pairing Sirius’s Crux software-defined networking platform with Arqit’s Sovereign Key Platform (SKP) and NetworkSecure adaptors, the companies provide symmetric, quantum-safe encryption across commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) devices, tactical radios, and uncrewed autonomous vehicles (UAVs) without requiring hardware overhauls.

Bridging the Gap Between Heavy QKD and Bandwidth-Constrained Edge Links
Frontline tactical operations present severe operating constraints. Hardware-dependent Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) cannot be routed over moving aircraft, tactical vehicles, or dismounted soldiers, while algorithmic Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) candidates can introduce processing latency and larger ciphertext sizes that strain narrowband tactical frequencies.
Sirius’s Crux platform overcomes these barriers by running as a lightweight software layer directly on existing edge devices. Arqit’s software-defined keys are dynamically injected into standard IPsec and transport-layer tunnels, allowing allied coalition forces to establish encrypted links across differing hardware standards without deploying specialized cryptographic appliances.
Sirius developed its foundational secure networking capabilities under the UK Government Communications Headquarters’ (HMGCC) Co-Creation programme. The capital infusion from Arqit will fund engineering expansion across Sirius’s facilities in the United States, United Kingdom, and Germany, alongside specialized hardware acceleration for autonomous defense platforms.
Commercial Momentum and Financial Trajectory
The investment in Sirius aligns with Arqit’s commercial transition toward defense alliances and automated key lifecycle management. In its preliminary financial reporting for fiscal year 2026, Arqit reported expected annual revenues near $1 million, supported by a cash and liquidity reserve of approximately $51 million.
Beyond the NATO contract win, Arqit confirmed commercial traction for its newly launched Encryption Intelligence platform, which inventories enterprise cryptographic assets and identifies legacy vulnerabilities ahead of mandated federal post-quantum deadlines.

Dana’s talk on OpenAI’s proof of the Unique Games Conjecture, now on YouTube!

Shtetl-Optimized Scott 3d quantum 340 words 2 min read

Enjoy all three hours of this! Dana covers what the Unique Games problem is and why people cared about it, the structure of a proof of its NP-hardness, exactly where the previous attempts to complete the proof (based on the Long Code and the Hadamard Code) got stuck, the new tree-based code that the AI discovered, and how the new code gets around the Long Code and Hadamard Code’s limitations. She doesn’t get to the soundness analysis.
Feel free to ask questions in the comments. I might write some notes here later with my own doofus understanding of Dana’s talk. Experts are of course hard at work on more formal writeups of the proof (which will hopefully be more comprehensible than the AI’s writeup).

IP Law Firm MBHB Launches Dedicated Quantum, Optics, and Semiconductors Practice Group in Chicago

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 10h quantum 199 words 1 min read

Combines engineering and national lab backgrounds (Purdue, Sandia, CINT) to evaluate complex hardware claims.
Technology Coverage
Photonic and solid-state QPUs, compound semiconductors, and integrated optics
Patent drafting, USPTO prosecution, and portfolio risk management.
Commercial Strategy
Startups, academic spin-outs, and Fortune 100 enterprises
Advises on Bayh-Dole compliance, joint research agreements, and international licensing.
The practice addresses growing patenting challenges as quantum innovation transitions from basic research to proprietary products. In particular, firms must navigate 35 U.S.C. § 101 eligibility by grounding claims in physical apparatuses—such as cryogenic controls and photonic packaging—rather than abstract algorithms, while managing federal march-in rights and export controls tied to public funding.

In the wake of the Riemannocalypse, here, for better or worse, is a very special video—unlike any I’ve done before—recorded just this weekend at Lighthaven in Berkeley. You get to watch Václav Rozhoň, of the “Polylog” YouTube channel, torture me by handing me sticky notes with numerous future milestones (P≠NP, P=BPP, the Collatz Conjecture, Busy Beaver 6 and 7, scalable quantum computers, nuclear fusion, quantum gravity, diseases cured, Dyson sphere around the Sun…), and defying me to place them on the table in the order in which I guess that AIs and/or humans will achieve them.

Here’s one that I’ll share even though I’m less happy about it. Jake Newfield hosted Robin Hanson, who I’ve known for decades, and me for a conversation about … well, I didn’t know about what until I showed up! But Robin came ready to argue that we, humans, should be 100% fine to go extinct and get replaced by robots, whatever values the robots turn out to pursue, and I’m a meat chauvinist if I believe otherwise, and why on earth should I worry that AI will kill me more than I worry that my own children, who also have values potentially different from mine, will kill me? I left feeling like crap, thinking Robin had had the better of the argument, because I’d been unable to muster any counterargument to him that didn’t essentially amount to “seriously? dude? WTF?” But then people overwhelmingly take my side in the YouTube comments (and criticize Robin for not letting me speak), so I guess there’s that!

NTT DOCOMO Deploys Second D-Wave Production Quantum Application to Optimize Telecom Network Signaling

Quantum Computing Report Mohamed Abdel-Kareem 4h quantum 295 words 2 min read

Japanese telecommunications operator NTT DOCOMO has deployed its second commercial, production-grade quantum application powered by D-Wave Quantum Inc. (NASDAQ: QBTS). Operating on D-Wave’s hybrid quantum annealing platform via the Leap™ cloud service, the application optimizes Tracking Area Lists (TA-Lists) across mobile networks, reducing daily peak location registration signaling by 65.3% and paging signaling load by 7.0%.

Multi-Objective Network Optimization
Telecommunication networks organize geographic base stations into Tracking Area Lists (TA-Lists) to manage device handoffs and call routing. Optimizing TA-Lists presents a complex combinatorial challenge because location registration signals (triggered when a device crosses tracking boundaries) and paging signals (broadcast to locate devices for incoming connections) move in opposite directions—reducing one typically increases the other.
- Benchmark Problem Size: In a operational benchmark spanning 333 base stations, three TA-Lists, and nine tracking areas, D-Wave’s hybrid quantum-classical solver executed the multi-objective optimization in approximately five minutes.
- Operational Integration: NTT DOCOMO has integrated the generated network configurations directly into its day-to-day network planning and live operational infrastructure, supporting over 93 million mobile subscriptions across Japan.
- Expanding Production Use Cases: This deployment builds on DOCOMO’s initial D-Wave production application, which targeted paging efficiency within individual tracking areas, expanding quantum annealing from isolated use cases to network-wide infrastructure optimization.
